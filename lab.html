The Self-Sufficiency Lab

Use AI with confidence. Move your career forward.

A practical learning program created by Atta Arghandiwal and Sohrab Rasouli, co-authors of The Self-Sufficient Immigrant in the Age of AI.

If you are new to the United States, returning to work, or trying to move into a role that better reflects your experience, the Lab helps you use AI for real career tasks without giving up your judgment, privacy, or voice.

Start with what you already know

Your experience still matters. The Lab helps you use it.

You do not need to become a technology expert. You need practical ways to use new tools for the work and decisions in front of you.

In the Lab, you practice using AI to strengthen a resume, prepare for job searches and interviews, write clear messages, compare options, and create a realistic plan for what comes next.

Co-author · Founder of Noura

Sohrab Rasouli

Sohrab Rasouli is an Afghan immigrant, the founder of Noura, and co-author of The Self-Sufficient Immigrant in the Age of AI. His path has taken him from a farming village in northern Afghanistan to the United Kingdom, Kabul, and the United States. After working as an educator, founding the Creative Academy, and serving newcomers as an Employment Specialist, he created Noura—a supervised AI work partner for employment-support teams. His work focuses on building practical tools that solve real problems while preserving human judgment and dignity.

Is the Lab for you?

The Lab meets you where you are.

The Lab is for immigrants, refugees, young adults, and people returning to work who want practical support using AI for employment, education, or career advancement.

ExperiencedYou have training, a degree, or years of experience, but your current role does not reflect what you can do.
Ages 18-26You are building confidence and choosing your first education or career path.
ReturningYou are coming back to the workforce and want a safe, practical way to use new tools.

What you will do

Learn in a small group. Practice with real career tasks.

01

Join a small group at a community organization or adult education program you know and trust.

02

Complete 15-25 hours of guided, hands-on learning over 5-8 weeks.

03

Use AI for resumes, job searches, interview preparation, messages, action plans, and everyday decisions.

04

Leave with practical AI skills, a working resume, a personal action plan, and a clear next step toward work or education.

The goal is not to make AI the expert. The goal is to help you use AI while keeping your own judgment in charge.

What you will leave with

Useful work you can keep using after the Lab.

Career tools you can use

  • A working resume you can continue to improve.
  • Stronger job-search and interview preparation.
  • A safer way to ask AI for help and check its answers.
  • More confidence using AI for real-world tasks.

Your personal next-step plan

You will identify the next action that makes sense for you, whether that is applying for work, improving a skill, continuing your education, or moving toward a role that better matches your experience.

What makes the Lab different

Human support comes first.

You learn in a small group, with guidance, time to ask questions, and room to practice at your own pace.

The Lab is hosted by community organizations and adult education programs so you can learn in a familiar setting. Every activity begins with a real need, not with technology for its own sake.

Built for real life

The Lab draws on workforce research, practical AI literacy guidance, and the framework developed in The Self-Sufficient Immigrant in the Age of AI. You learn when AI can help, when to slow down, and when human judgment must come first.
