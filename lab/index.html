AI Self-Sufficiency Lab

Use AI safely. Make something useful. Leave with a plan.

A practical three-session Lab for immigrants, students, families, and community groups—co-led by Atta Arghandiwal and Sohrab Rasouli.

Now inviting first host and sponsor conversations.

Bring the Lab to your community→See the three sessions→

The three sessions

A short path with something to show at the end.

A three-session guided experience that helps immigrants, students, families, and community members use AI more safely, create one useful output, and leave with a practical 30-day plan.

  1. 01

    Judgment and safety

    The first session is about deciding what a tool is for before using it. Participants set one goal of their own, learn where a person has to stay in charge, and write a first prompt they can use safely.

    You leave withSafety Card, one goal, first safe prompt.

  2. 02

    One useful output

    The second session produces something real — a letter, a plan, a summary, a set of questions — then checks it. Participants verify what the tool produced and revise it in their own words.

    You leave withFirst draft, verification checklist, participant's own revision.

  3. 03

    Present and plan

    In the third session each participant presents what they made and leaves with a plan they can act on without the group.

    You leave withRevised output, short presentation, 30-day plan.

Atta Arghandiwal

How it runs

Small groups, clear limits.

10–20 participants
Length
Three sessions, each up to 90 minutes
Virtual

In person

Travel elsewhere
Only with a written travel and expense agreement
Price
Agreed in writing for each host. No public price.

Responsibilities

What each side brings.

What the host provides

  • The group and one planning contact.
  • An accessible room or virtual setting.
  • Participant coordination and known language or accessibility needs.
  • The agreed tools, accounts, schedule, and written terms.

What Atta and Sohrab provide

  • Three guided sessions of up to 90 minutes.
  • Exercises and participant materials.
  • The Safety Card, a checked useful output, and a 30-day plan.
  • The stated privacy, verification, and human-judgment boundaries.

Boundaries

What this is not.

These limits are stated in the room, in the materials, and here, because they are the point of the Lab rather than a disclaimer at the end of it.

  • AI is not a lawyer, doctor, immigration adviser, tax adviser, lender, employer, or final decision-maker.
  • Do not enter private identity, financial, medical, immigration, employment, or confidential documents into public AI tools.
  • Check important information with official sources or qualified people.
  • No employment, income, admission, funding, loan, immigration, or business outcome is promised.

Who hosts a Lab

Built to sit inside an organization people already trust.
