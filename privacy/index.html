Privacy

What happens to what you type here.

Last updated July 2026.

Right now, nothing is collected

This is a preview of the AttaMoves website. The forms on it are demonstrations. They are not connected to any service. When you fill one in and press the button, the page changes to show you what a confirmation will look like — and that is all that happens.

Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere. Nothing is written to a database, a spreadsheet, an inbox, or a log. Nothing is stored in your browser after you close the tab.

There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking

This site loads no analytics, no advertising scripts, no tracking pixels, and no third-party fonts. It sets no cookies. Nothing about your visit is measured or shared.

Everything the page needs — text, images, fonts — comes from this site itself. The only requests that leave it are ones you start by clicking a link to a bookshop, a social profile, or a recording. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this one has no control over them.

Email is different

If you write to atta@attamoves.com or attamoves@gmail.com, that email arrives in an ordinary mailbox and is read by a person. It is kept in that mailbox in the normal way. If you would like a message deleted, ask and it will be.

What will change when the forms are connected

A later release will connect the inquiry forms to a real service so that requests reach Atta and Sohrab reliably. When that happens, this page will be rewritten before the change goes live, and it will say exactly which service is used, what is stored, for how long, and how to ask for a copy or a deletion.

The intended rule is simple: a form will collect only what is needed to answer the request — who you are, how to reach you, and what you are asking about — and nothing more.

How a request will be handled

Requests will be reviewed privately by Atta and Sohrab. AI may be used to summarise a long request or to prepare a first draft of a reply, as a working aid.

A person approves every message, every fee, every date, every acceptance, and every public change. Nothing is booked, priced, accepted, or promised automatically. No reply is sent without a human reading it first.

The newsletter is not connected either

The Stay connected form is also a preview. There is no mailing list behind it yet, so nothing you enter creates a subscription. When a real list exists, it will have explicit consent, a confirmation step, a working unsubscribe link in every message, and this page will describe it.

Questions

Write to atta@attamoves.com. A person will answer.
