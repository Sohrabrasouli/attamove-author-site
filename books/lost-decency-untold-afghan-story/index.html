About this book

About Lost Decency: The Untold Afghan Story

Atta's original memoir follows his family and the Afghan people through the loss of a once-peaceful homeland, escape, displacement, and the long search for belonging. Blending personal memory with Afghanistan's turbulent modern history, it records what exile takes from a person and what dignity, family, and moral memory can help preserve.

Why read this

Read this edition to understand the original memoir path that first introduced Atta's story of Afghanistan, exile, dignity, and home.

Best for

Readers who already know the original title, collectors, libraries, and biography researchers.
