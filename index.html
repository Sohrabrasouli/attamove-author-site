Atta Recommends

Start with structure, not everything at once.

You do not have to rebuild your whole life in one day. Begin with responsibility: documents, work, family stability, judgment, and the daily habits that protect dignity.

Best starting point

Start with The Self-Sufficient Immigrant in the Age of AI, then move to The Self-Sufficient Global Citizen when you want a wider roadmap.
