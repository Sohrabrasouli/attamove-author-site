What this site is

attamoves.com presents the books, talks, and community work of Atta Arghandiwal. This build is a preview: some parts are complete and some are still being prepared, and the site says so where that is the case.

The writing is for reading, not for advice

The books, talks, and pages here describe experience and offer practical thinking. They are not legal, immigration, medical, financial, tax, or employment advice, and they are not a substitute for a qualified person who knows your situation.

The AI Self-Sufficiency Lab teaches judgement about tools. It does not promise any employment, income, admission, funding, loan, immigration, or business outcome, and it says so in the room as well as here.

The forms do not create an agreement

Sending an inquiry — by form or by email — starts a conversation. It does not book a date, agree a fee, accept an engagement, or create an obligation on either side. Nothing is confirmed until you receive a written reply that says so.

In this preview the forms are demonstrations and send nothing at all.

Books and retailers

Books are sold by independent retailers. Prices, formats, editions, and availability are theirs to set and can change without notice. Links here point to the destinations recorded as correct at the time of writing; if one is wrong, please say so and it will be fixed.

Photographs and text

The photographs on this site come from Atta's personal and family record and remain his. Book covers belong to their publishers. The text on this site is his work. None of it may be reproduced commercially without permission — but quoting a passage with attribution, or asking for an approved photograph for an article, is welcome. Ask at atta@attamoves.com.

Links to other sites

Links to retailers, recordings, and social platforms are provided because they are useful. Those sites are not controlled from here and their content and policies are their own.

Changes

This site is being built in stages, and these terms will be updated as services are connected. The version in front of you describes the site as it is today.
