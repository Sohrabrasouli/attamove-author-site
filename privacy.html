Privacy

Privacy and contact information.

This site is designed as a public author, speaking, media, and book information site for Atta Arghandiwal.

What is collected

The site does not use a backend contact database. Contact forms, inquiry buttons, and the first readers list open your email app so you can send a message directly to attamoves@gmail.com. If you choose to join the first readers list, the message may include your name, email, area of interest, optional phone number, and optional note.

Retail and external links

Book, media, and social links may take you to third-party websites such as retailers, video platforms, media outlets, or social networks. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

Updates

If you ask to receive updates, your email may be used to reply about books, launch news, speaking, interviews, or related work. If you provide a phone number, it may be used for optional text reminders. You can ask to stop receiving updates at any time.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to attamoves@gmail.com.
